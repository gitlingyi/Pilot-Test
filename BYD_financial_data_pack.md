## 4. Management Discussion And Guidance

> Distilled from FY2025 annual report (chairman's statement / MD&A, pp. 7–40), 2025 interim report MD&A, and monthly sales-volume announcements. No verbatim filing paragraphs.

### 4.1 Quarter / Year Headline
FY2025: revenue RMB 804.0bn (+3.5%), net profit attributable RMB 32.62bn (**−18.97%**), the first annual profit decline after years of rapid growth. **Q1 2026 deteriorated sharply**: revenue RMB 150.2bn (−11.8% YoY), net profit attributable RMB 4.08bn (−55.4% YoY). Gross margin slipped FY2024 19.4% → FY2025 17.7% → Q1'26 18.8% (vs Q1'25 20.1%).

### 4.2 Business Line / Segment (FY2025)
- **Automobiles & related products: RMB 648.65bn, +5.06% YoY, 80.68% of revenue.**
- **Mobile handset components, assembly & other: RMB 155.24bn, −2.74% YoY, 19.31% of revenue.**
- Auto segment growth (+5%) far below prior years; electronics segment contracted. Δ flag: **decelerating**.

### 4.3 Margin & Cost Story
Margin compression is the central FY2025 story — management attributes it to **intense domestic price competition** ("price war") in China NEVs. Despite revenue growth, gross profit fell 5.6% and operating profit fell 20.4%. **R&D rose to RMB 57.98bn** (expensed) / RMB 63.4bn total investment (+9% area), a structurally high ~7.2% of revenue, pressuring margins further. Operating cash flow collapsed (133.5bn → 59.1bn) on **working-capital outflows** (inventories +22.4bn, trade payables −54.9bn) while **capex surged 61% to RMB 156.8bn** — capacity + overseas plant build-out.

### 4.4 Capital & Liquidity
Cash & equivalents (monetary funds) RMB 75.4bn; broader "cash reserves" RMB 167.8bn. **Financing inflow RMB 104.6bn in FY2025** — borrowings drawn RMB 134.0bn, capital injections RMB 40.2bn (incl. the **March-2025 H-share placement**, ~HK$43.5bn), perpetual-instrument issuance. Long-term borrowings jumped 8.26bn → 60.71bn. FY2025 final **dividend RMB 0.358/share** (tax incl.) on the post-bonus 9.117bn-share base; **July-2025 bonus issue** tripled the share count. Treasury shares RMB 3.99bn.

### 4.5 Tone Shift vs Prior Periods
Clear narrative shift from "rapid growth" to **"steady growth amid intense competition."** First annual profit decline (FY2025) and an accelerating top-line decline into 2026 (Q1'26 revenue −11.8%; **Jan–Apr 2026 NEV unit sales −26.0% YoY**). New first-disclosure emphasis on **globalization/exports and brand premiumization** as the growth engine, offsetting a **domestic BEV volume decline (−7.91% FY2025)**.

### 4.7 Forward Guidance — Acceptance Gate

**"No formal quantitative guidance" policy caveat.** As a China A-share + HK-listed issuer, BYD does **not** publish forward EPS/revenue guidance ranges in the US-style sense. Its forward-looking disclosure consists of: (a) qualitative management outlook in the annual/interim MD&A (continued globalization, premiumization, technology investment), and (b) the **monthly production & sales-volume voluntary announcements**, which function as the market's high-frequency forward indicator. The 2026 YTD volume trend (−26% units) is the most current quantitative forward signal. No quantitative profit/revenue outlook table is issued by the company — this is an **issuer-policy gap, not a data-collection failure**.
